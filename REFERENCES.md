# Verified citations

Checked via web search 2026-10-26, not from memory. Use these exact
details in the paper's bibliography.

## Adversarial debiasing (primary baseline/foundation)
Zhang, B. H., Lemoine, B., & Mitchell, M. (2018). Mitigating Unwanted
Biases with Adversarial Learning. In *Proceedings of the 2018 AAAI/ACM
Conference on AI, Ethics, and Society (AIES)*.
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.07593 (posted 2018-01-22)
- Note: author order differs slightly between the arXiv listing (Zhang
  first) and Google's own listing (Lemoine first) — use the AIES
  proceedings order (Zhang, Lemoine, Mitchell) as the canonical one.

## Post-processing equalized odds (the method that won the comparison)
Hardt, M., Price, E., & Srebro, N. (2016). Equality of Opportunity in
Supervised Learning. In *Advances in Neural Information Processing
Systems 29 (NeurIPS/NIPS 2016)*, pp. 3315-3323.
- https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/6374-equality-of-opportunity-in-supervised-learning
- Conference was called NIPS in 2016, commonly cited as NeurIPS now.

## COMPAS dataset / motivating case study
Angwin, J., Larson, J., Mattu, S., & Kirchner, L. (2016, May 23). Machine
Bias. *ProPublica*.
- https://www.propublica.org/article/machine-bias-risk-assessments-in-criminal-sentencing
- Published 2016-05-23 (verified against ProQuest reprint; a UCR archive
  mirror shows a March 2016 date but that's the mirror's own posting
  date, not the original publication date).

## Gradient-reversal joint training (ablation)
Ganin, Y., & Lempitsky, V. (2015). Unsupervised Domain Adaptation by
Backpropagation. In *Proceedings of the 32nd International Conference on
Machine Learning (ICML)*, PMLR vol. 37, pp. 1180-1189.
- https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/ganin15.html
- arXiv preprint (if needed): arXiv:1409.7495

## Lagrangian/dual-ascent framing for fairness constraints (Method section)
Agarwal, A., Beygelzimer, A., Dudík, M., Langford, J., & Wallach, H.
(2018). A Reductions Approach to Fair Classification. In *Proceedings of
the 35th International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML)*, PMLR vol.
80, pp. 60-69.
- https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/agarwal18a.html

Cotter, A., Jiang, H., Gupta, M., Wang, S., Narayan, T., You, S., &
Sridharan, K. (2019). Optimization with Non-Differentiable Constraints
with Applications to Fairness, Recall, Churn, and Other Goals. *Journal
of Machine Learning Research*, 20(172), 1-59.
- https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v20/18-616.html
- CAUTION: do not confuse with Cotter, Jiang & Sridharan's separate "Two-
  Player Games for Efficient Non-Convex Constrained Optimization" (ALT
  2019, PMLR vol. 98, pp. 300-332) — different paper, different author
  list (no Gupta), easy to conflate since both are Cotter + constrained
  optimization. Use the JMLR one above for the Gupta-coauthored citation.
